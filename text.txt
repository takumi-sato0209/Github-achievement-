Your list mixes verb forms ("explore" vs "exploration" vs "exploring") with nouns. Combined naively, some branches come out as awkward English:

"sojourn-ascending-explorer" (noun + verb-ing + noun) — acceptable

"exploration-journey-wanderer" (noun + noun + noun) — acceptable

"sojourning-pilgrimage-nomad" — nonsense

If the generated names are only ever used as identifiers (which they seem to be — they're for achievement branches), you don't care. They'll look like random codenames regardless.

If you actually want readable English phrases, add a filter that rejects any combination where word_2 ends in -ing and word_3 is a noun — but for a bot-generated branch name this is overkill.
